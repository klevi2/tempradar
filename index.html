How the numbers are made

  • Consensus is the average daily high of up to nine weather models (ECMWF IFS and AIFS, GFS, ICON, GEM, UK Met Office, Météo-France, JMA, and NBM for US stations) for the station’s local calendar day, at the station’s coordinates and elevation.
  • Bucket chances come from 161 ensemble members (ECMWF 51, GEFS 31, ICON 40, GEM 21, MOGREPS-G 18). Each member’s daily high is rounded to a whole degree, halves up, and counts once. Fahrenheit buckets are two degrees wide and start on an even number (80–81); Celsius buckets are one degree.
  • Today so far (US stations) is read from api.weather.gov. Following the Polymarket rule, only routine METARs count, using the T-group in tenths of a degree Celsius, converted and rounded halves up. On the Today view, members below the measured high are counted at the measured high.

Limits

  • A model grid cell is not the airport thermometer. Coastal, lake-side and mountain stations can be several degrees off.
  • Ensemble daily highs are built from hourly or three-hourly values and tend to miss short peaks. The spread is raw, not calibrated.
  • Kalshi lists The Weather Company as its source and its bucket edges move from day to day; use the bucket-start switch in a Kalshi row to match the day’s listing.
  • Stations were checked against the open markets on . Rules change, so read the rules of the market you trade.
